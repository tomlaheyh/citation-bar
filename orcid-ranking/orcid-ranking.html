Years are by PubMed entry date, not publication year, and the latest year is partial; its months are shown beside the year. Every ORCID on a record is counted, whatever the author's position. Names are kept only for first, second and last authors, so a name can be blank. People tied at the cut-off share the rank, so a year can list more than ten. Counts come from a local copy of PubMed.
