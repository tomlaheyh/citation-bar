This is a demo, not live PubMed. A real PubMed results page frozen on a recent date, with the actual Citation Bar running on it (extension version —). Everything on the bar works — hover any item for what it means, open an abstract, the MeSH terms and keywords, an author's ORCID and h-index, the journal panel (J) or the monthly citation chart beside Cited, switch the bar's language with the EN tag, tick the green check to build an export list and download it as .nbib or .ris, or build an AI pack (.zip) for an AI tool. Citation counts and scores are frozen at the snapshot date, and searching is disabled.

The bar you are looking at is the extension's own code, unmodified, running over a saved page. The article records, abstracts, MeSH terms, author and journal details and monthly citation counts were captured at the snapshot date and are bundled with the page. The one thing fetched live is an article's free full text, from Europe PMC or PubMed Central, when you open its abstract or build an AI pack. Convert PDF reads a PDF from your own computer inside your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Two things can't be shown here because they need a live search: Xout, which rebuilds your query to exclude reviews, editorials and retractions, and Scan 1,000, which scores a thousand results and returns the highest-impact ones.
