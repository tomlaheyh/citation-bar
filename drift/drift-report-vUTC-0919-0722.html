The +0 column holds the locked absolute value. Every column after it is that measure's cumulative change from the locked number, never a day-over-day step — so a single failed pull leaves one hole rather than corrupting everything after it. Colour carries direction only; the number carries the amount. Hover any cell for the absolute value behind the drift.

The two pinned columns are different. YTD is the whole year to the close date shown, and its drift is one day's change — the same range measured on two consecutive mornings, so the window is identical and nothing of the change comes from the year getting longer. The columns to its right are cumulative from an anchor. Don't read across the divider as if it were one scale.
